Research Interests

Bridging first-principles physics, geophysical observations, and numerical simulation in solid-Earth dynamics.

Lithosphere & Mantle Rheology

How the lithosphere and mantle deform, constrained jointly by numerical models and geophysical observations: power-law creep, dislocation vs. diffusion regimes, and viscosity–temperature–stress feedbacks.

Earthquake Physics & Fault Mechanics

Fault mechanics, earthquake-cycle processes, and what they imply for seismic hazard.

Geodesy, Seismology & Physics-Based Models

Integrating geodetic (GNSS/InSAR) and seismic observations with physics-based numerical modeling.

Active Tectonics of Southeast Asia

Active tectonics, stress and strain partitioning, and earthquake hazards across Southeast Asia.

Research Projects

Current and past research, from mantle geodynamics to geothermal exploration.

M.S. — Galápagos Plume–Ridge Interaction (2024–2026)

3D numerical modeling of plume–ridge interaction with complex (power-law) rheology and partial melting, using LaMEM on high-performance computing. Member of the Marine IGUANA ocean-bottom seismometer project. Advisor: Garrett Apuzen-Ito, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa.

B.S. — Curie Point Depth of Southern Thailand (2022–2023)

Curie point depth and heat-flow mapping from airborne magnetic data for geothermal exploration. Advisors: Pitsanupong Kanjanapayont (Chulalongkorn University) and Nicole Lautze (University of Hawaiʻi).

Geothermal Systems of Southern Thailand (2020–2024)

Field-led electrical resistivity tomography and hot-spring geochemistry in Ranong and Surat Thani, integrating structural geology, geophysics, and geochemistry.
